# Choosing a Story

![Story Planning](img/2_sot_story_planning.svg)

**Why:** Work is pulled, not pushed. The team takes one story at a time, always from the top, so the product manager's ordering is what gets built.

**Who:** 👱 🤖 either a person or an agent.

**In:** The prioritized backlog. A signal that the work queue is empty, after [Deploy](6-deploy-accept-release.md).

**Out:** One story, sent to [Updating Evals](3-updating-evals.md).

## How

1. When the work queue is empty, take the story at the top of the backlog.
2. If the backlog is empty, go back to [Product Planning](1-product-planning.md) to choose the next epic.

Don't skip ahead in the backlog. If the top story isn't ready, that is a planning problem — fix the order, not the pick.

## Raise an issue

Raise an issue when the top story depends on work that hasn't been done yet.
