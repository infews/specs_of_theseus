# Product Planning

![Product Planning](img/1_sot_product_planning.svg)

**Why:** The team needs one ordered list of work, and a person who owns its order. This is the Pivotal Labs or XP planning loop, with an agent helping to break epics down.

**Who:** 👱 the product manager leads. 🤖 an agent helps break down epics and suggest an order. Developers are involved to vet the agent's work

**In:** Ideas. Stories rejected at [Acceptance](6-deploy-accept-release.md).

**Out:** A prioritized backlog of stories.

## How

1. **Product Prioritization** 👱 — rank ideas into prioritized work. Each epic is a collection or work that is releaseable at once to customers.
2. **Choose Epic** 👱 — pick the next epic to break down and potentially start.
3. **Epic Breakdown** 👱 🤖 — split the epic into small, independent stories, each with acceptance criteria.
4. **Prioritization** 👱 🤖 — order the stories into the backlog. Rejected stories come back here and are ranked again against all planned work.

When the backlog runs dry, [Choosing a Story](2-choosing-a-story.md) sends the team back to choose the next epic.

## Raise an issue

An agent should raise an issue when an epic can't be split into stories that can each be accepted on their own, or when a story's acceptance criteria are too vague to write evals from.
